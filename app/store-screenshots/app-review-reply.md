2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Deals More helps shoppers in Pakistan find brand sales, promo codes and bank card discounts at restaurants and stores in one place. Offers are usually spread across brand emails, websites, social media and bank websites, so people miss them. The app collects these publicly announced offers, organizes them by brand, category and bank card, and notifies users about new offers from brands they follow or when a tracked product reaches their target discount.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE FEATURES
No login is needed for the main features: tap "Skip" on the sign-in screen to browse as a guest.
For features that need an account (followed brands, sale alerts and account deletion), please use the demo account in the App Review Information section.

Steps:
1. Tap "Get Started", pick some interests and categories, then sign in with the demo account or tap Skip.
2. Home: browse deals and coupons; tap one to see details and the promo code.
3. Explore: browse coupons by brand; filter by In-Store, Online or Expiring Soon.
4. Bank Offers: filter places by category, city or bank; tap a place to see eligible cards.
5. Track: paste a product link from a supported store, set a target discount and create an alert.
6. Profile: preferences, followed brands, notifications, Privacy Policy, Terms and Delete Account.
No sample files are needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Our own backend API (hosted on Render, PostgreSQL): deals, brands, bank offers and sale alerts
- Firebase Authentication, Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In: accounts
- Firebase Cloud Messaging / APNs: notifications
- Microsoft Clarity: anonymous usage analytics
- Google Gemini and Groq: used only on our server to extract deal details (brand, discount, code, expiry) from brands' promotional announcements. Users do not interact with these models.
- Apple Maps / Google Maps: opened when the user taps Near Me or a branch address
- Public brand and bank websites and newsletters: source of offer information
The app does not process payments.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions and shows the same content to every user. Content currently covers Pakistan (Pakistani brands, stores and banks). No features are restricted by region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Deals More is not a bank or payment service. It does not process payments, access bank accounts or store full card numbers. Bank card offers are informational listings of discounts that banks advertise publicly.
Brand names, logos and offers come from the brands' own public promotions and are shown only to identify where a deal is available. We are not affiliated with these brands or banks, and we remove content promptly at a rights holder's request through our support contact on the App Store listing.
Sale alerts, tracked links and "Request a Brand" submissions are private and never shown to other users.
